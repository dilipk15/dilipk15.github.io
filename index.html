FAQ

Common questions

Can't find your answer? Send me a message.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on the scope, so I give a fixed price after a short call rather than a vague range. You always know the full price before agreeing to anything.

You're in India. How does working together work?

We talk over video call and email. I'm available during UK afternoons, US mornings and Australian afternoons, and can arrange other times when needed.

Who owns the website, code and data?

You do. Everything is set up in your own accounts where possible, and all code and documentation are handed over when the project is paid.

What happens after the project is finished?

You get 30 days of free fixes. After that you can come back for changes whenever you need them, or ask about a small monthly support plan.

Do I need to use Google Workspace?

No. Automations are often built on Google Workspace because many small businesses already use it, but websites and web applications work with whatever email and office tools you use.

Have a process that eats up your week?

Tell me about it. I reply within one working day, and the first call is free.
