Hi, I'm Dilip

A software developer based in India, helping small businesses with websites, automation and web apps.

My story

Building systems people use every day

Most of my career has been spent building portals, dashboards and a lot of automation. I've built more than 400 Google Apps Script automations, and a research internship portal that has handled over 80,000 applications. For the past few years I've also led a small development team.

The work I enjoy most quietly removes a chore. Twenty-four separate forms become one portal. A weekly report nobody has to put together by hand anymore. A reminder that goes out without anyone having to remember it.

Small businesses deal with exactly these problems, usually without an IT team. That's who I work with now: owners who would rather spend their time on customers than on admin.
