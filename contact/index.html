FAQ

Before you get in touch

Is the first call free?

Yes. The first call is free and there's no obligation. It's mainly a chance to understand what you need and whether I'm the right person for it.

Do I need to know exactly what I want?

No. Describe the problem in your own words, for example "we lose track of quotes", and we'll work out the solution together.

Can you sign an NDA?

Yes, happy to sign a reasonable NDA before you share anything sensitive.

Can you work with my time zone?

Yes. I work with clients in the UK, US, Canada, Australia and New Zealand, and arrange calls at a time that suits you. Day to day most things happen over email, so you are never waiting on a call to make progress.
