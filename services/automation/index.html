FAQ

Questions about business automation

What kind of work can be automated?

Anything repetitive that follows the same steps: sending quotes and invoices, reminders, confirmations, moving information from forms into sheets, building reports, creating documents from templates. On a short call we can work out what's worth automating first.

Do I need Google Workspace?

Many automations are built on Google Workspace because lots of small businesses already use Gmail and Google Sheets. If you use other tools, we can often connect them through their own integrations.

What happens if something stops working?

You get 30 days of free fixes after handover, a plain-English guide, and a log that shows what the automation did. After that, I'm available for changes or a small monthly support plan.

Is my data safe?

Automations run inside your own accounts, so your data stays with you. I only need access while building, and you can remove it afterwards.

Will there be new monthly fees?

Usually not. Most automations run on tools you already pay for. If a paid service would genuinely be better, I'll tell you and explain why.

Have a process that eats up your week?

Tell me about it. I reply within one working day, and the first call is free.
